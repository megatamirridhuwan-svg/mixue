About MIXUE

From a small shop to a global phenomenon — happiness in every cup.

A Taste of Joy, Brewed with Passion

MIXUE began with a simple belief — that everyone deserves a little happiness in a cup. What started as a modest tea shop has grown into one of the world's most loved ice cream and tea brands.

We combine premium ingredients, time-honored recipes and a playful spirit to create drinks and desserts that brighten your day, one sip and one scoop at a time.
